## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The map fills the screen, with the panel coming up as a sheet from the bottom rather than
      taking a column beside it
- [ ] The three panel icons stay reachable along the bottom edge whether the sheet is open or shut
- [ ] Dismissing the sheet gives the whole screen back to the map
- [ ] The selected token appears as a bar pinned above the icons, only when something is selected —
      it isn't in the sheet, and it doesn't hold space when nothing is selected
- [ ] The controls for the active tool are reachable without horizontal scrolling on a bar that
      floats over the map
- [ ] Every action available on a desktop is reachable on a phone, even if it takes an extra tap
