As a Room Member
I want to see who is currently connected to the room
So that I know who's actually at the table right now, not just who has ever joined

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The panel shows a list of currently connected Room Members, updated in real time as people connect/disconnect
- [ ] The list is visible regardless of which tab (chat/initiative tracker) is active
- [ ] Someone who joined previously but isn't connected right now doesn't appear in the list
