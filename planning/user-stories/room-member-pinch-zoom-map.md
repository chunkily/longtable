As a Room Member
I want to pinch to zoom the map on a tablet or phone
So that I can play from the device I actually brought to the table, instead of only being able to
see the handful of squares that fit on screen at 1:1

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A two-finger pinch zooms the map in and out
- [ ] The map stays put under my fingers while I pinch — the point I started on doesn't drift
- [ ] Zoom is bounded by the same limits the mouse wheel already respects
- [ ] The grid, measurements, the selection ring and the eraser's halo all stay the right size on
      screen after a pinch, exactly as they do after a wheel zoom
- [ ] One finger still pans when no tool is in use
- [ ] Starting a pinch while a tool is in use doesn't leave half a drawing, a half-painted fog
      sweep, or a measurement stranded on everyone else's map
