As a Room Member
I want the delete buttons out of the way until I want one, and the days marked
So that the panel reads as a conversation rather than a list of controls, and I can tell last
week's session from tonight's

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No delete button is visible until I hover, focus or tap the message it belongs to
- [ ] A message I can't delete never shows one at all
- [ ] Tapping a message on a touch screen reveals its button; the same tap can't delete anything
- [ ] I can still reach the button with a keyboard
- [ ] The first entry of each day has the date above it
- [ ] Today's date reads `Today` and the day before reads `Yesterday`
