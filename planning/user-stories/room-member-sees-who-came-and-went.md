As a Room Member
I want the chat log to show the time of each entry and to record people arriving and leaving
So that I can follow what happened while I was looking at the map, and pick a session back up
where it left off

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every chat entry shows the time it was posted
- [ ] The full date and time is available without leaving the panel
- [ ] Someone joining the room puts a line in the log for everyone already there
- [ ] Someone leaving puts a line in the log
- [ ] A connection blip puts nothing in the log
- [ ] Those lines survive a refresh, and are there for someone who joins later
- [ ] They are visibly not somebody's speech
