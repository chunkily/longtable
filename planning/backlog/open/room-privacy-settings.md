title: Room privacy settings (visibility + join password)
created: 2026-07-29
tags: [rooms]
---

Let a GM control who can find and join their room: public/private visibility (private by
default, so it isn't listed anywhere but the direct link) and an optional join password
independent of the GM password.

**Where it surfaces is now decided.** These settings live under `Manage room`, the third entry in
the side panel's menu — see [full-bleed-map-layout](full-bleed-map-layout.md). It shares that
container with [delete-room](delete-room.md) and the owner-only movement toggle from
[token-move-ownership-lock](token-move-ownership-lock.md), so whoever builds the first of the
three builds the container.
